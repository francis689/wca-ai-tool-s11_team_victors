RUPSA SACCO - SAVINGS PRODUCTS

Non-Withdrawable Savings

- Minimum contribution: Ksh 500 per month
- Withdrawals are not allowed.
- Loans can be issued against deposits.

Elimu Bora Savings

- Designed for educational expenses.
- Members can deposit daily or monthly.
- Withdrawals are allowed twice a year.
- Interest: 5% per annum, pro rata.

Term Deposits

- Flexible saving option.
- Withdrawal is allowed once per month.

Holiday Savings Account

- Members can save daily.
- Withdrawal is allowed once a year.
- Interest: 5% per annum, pro rata.
