RUPSA SACCO - LOAN PRODUCTS

General Loan Eligibility Requirements

A member should:
- Have saved for at least six months.
- Maintain a good credit history with the Sacco.
- Submit loan forms before the 5th of every month for processing.

Emergency Loan
- Maximum amount: Ksh 200,000
- Interest: 1.3% monthly
- Repayment period: 24 months

School Fees Loan
- Maximum amount: Ksh 200,000
- Interest: 1.15% monthly
- Repayment period: 12 months
- Intended for members or dependents.

Normal Loan
- Maximum: Up to 3 times the member's shares
- Interest: 1.0% monthly
- Repayment period: 36 months

Super Loan
- Maximum: Up to 4 times member deposits
- Interest: 1.2% monthly
- Repayment period: 60 months
- Requirements include not being a first loan, no defaults,
  guarantors, and minimum deposits of Ksh 200,000.

Pamoja Loan
- Maximum: Up to 5 times savings
- Interest: 1.2% monthly
- Repayment period: 60 months

Asset Financing / Car Loan
- Maximum multiplier: Up to 3 or 4 times
- Interest: 1.2% monthly
- Repayment period: 48 months
- No guarantors needed.

Insurance Loan
- Based on insurance premium
- Interest: 1.2% monthly
- Repayment period: 12 months

Product Loan
- Amount determined by product value
- Interest: 1.2% monthly
- Repayment period: 18 months

M Cash Mobile Loan
- Maximum amount: Ksh 50,000
- Interest: 5% charged upfront
- Repayment: within 1 month
- Launched in May 2023 according to the official FAQ.

Land and Construction Loan
- Maximum: Up to 3 or 4 times member deposits
- Interest: 1.4% monthly
- Repayment period: 72 months

Kilimo Loan
- Maximum: Up to 3 or 4 times member deposits
- Interest: 1.4% monthly

Loan Security

- Active membership is required.
- Consistent monthly savings are required.
- Adequate guarantors may be required.
- Self-guaranteed loans can use member deposits/savings as security.

Loan Processing

Loan processing depends on complete documentation, guarantors where required,
and fulfillment of loan conditions.

Emergency loans are generally processed faster, within 24 hours.

Early repayment is allowed.
