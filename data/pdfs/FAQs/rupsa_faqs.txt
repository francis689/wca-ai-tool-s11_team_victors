RUPSA SACCO - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CONTACT

Q: How can I contact RUPSA SACCO customer care?
A: Phone +254 717 604010 or +254 738 775754.
   Email: info@rupsasacco.com.

Q: Where is RUPSA SACCO located?
A: PCEA Ruiru Plaza, Ruiru-Kamiti Road, next to Ruiru Market.

PAYMENTS

Q: What is the RUPSA SACCO M-PESA Paybill?
A: 834700.

Q: What should I use as the account number when paying through Paybill?
A: Use your Member Number as the account number.

MEMBERSHIP

Q: What is the minimum initial contribution?
A: Ksh 4,000.

Q: What is the minimum monthly contribution?
A: Ksh 500.

Q: What is the required share capital?
A: Ksh 10,000 for individual members.

LOANS

Q: How long should I have saved before applying for a loan?
A: At least six months, subject to the specific loan product.

Q: Can I repay my loan early?
A: Yes. Early repayment is allowed.

Q: What happens if I default?
A: The SACCO may recover from guarantors, deposits, or take other
measures according to SACCO policies.

SAVINGS

Q: What savings products are available?
A: Non-Withdrawable Savings, Elimu Bora Savings, Term Deposits,
and Holiday Savings.

DIVIDENDS

Q: Are dividends paid to members?
A: Yes. Dividends are paid annually based on profits and approval
at the Annual General Meeting.

EXITING THE SACCO

Q: How do I exit the SACCO?
A: Submit a written exit request and observe the required 60-day
notice period. Outstanding obligations must be settled.

ONLINE SERVICES

Q: Does RUPSA SACCO have mobile banking?
A: Yes. Members can use USSD *879# and the online member portal.

USSD SERVICES INCLUDE:
- Balance inquiry
- Deposits
- PIN changes
- Loan application
- Loan repayment
- Mini statement
