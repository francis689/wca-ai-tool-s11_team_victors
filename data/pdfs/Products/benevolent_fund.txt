RUPSA SACCO - BENEVOLENT FUND

Purpose:
The Benevolent Fund helps with funeral arrangements in case of
the demise of a member or spouse.

Annual Premium:
Ksh 1,000 per member per year.

Benefit:
In case of a member's demise, the family is given Ksh 30,000
for burial expenses.

According to the official website:
- Loans are paid by insurance.
- The next of kin is refunded member savings.

Claim Procedure:
1. Fill in the claim form.
2. Attach the original and a copy of the burial permit.
3. Attach the ID of the member and deceased.
4. To facilitate final dues, provide the original death certificate.

Source:
Official RUPSA SACCO Benevolent Fund page.
