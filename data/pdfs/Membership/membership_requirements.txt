RUPSA SACCO - MEMBERSHIP INFORMATION

Who Can Join

Primary Members:
- Members of PCEA congregations
- Spouses of PCEA congregation members
- Employees of PCEA institutions
- Business persons
- Employees within Kiambu and Nairobi
- Diaspora members

Associate Members:
- Direct relatives of primary members
- Employees of organizations with formal agreements with RUPSA SACCO

How to Apply

An applicant should:
1. Complete the official RUPSA SACCO membership application form.
2. Provide two recent passport-size photographs.
3. Provide a copy of a National ID or valid passport.
4. Provide a copy of the KRA PIN certificate.
5. Provide any additional documents required for the specific membership category.

Initial Contribution

A minimum contribution of Ksh 4,000 is required upon joining.

The membership page states that this includes:
- Ksh 1,000 non-refundable entrance fee
- Ksh 1,000 Benevolent Fund fee paid annually

Share Capital

- Minimum share capital for individual members: Ksh 10,000
- Initial share capital upon joining: Ksh 2,000
- The remaining amount should be paid within 6 months.
- Share capital is non-refundable.
- Share capital can be transferred to another member.
- Share capital earns dividends at the end of the financial year.

Deposits Contribution

- Minimum monthly contribution: Ksh 500
- Deposits are non-withdrawable.
- Deposits can act as collateral for loans.
- Deposits earn interest annually.

Member Benefits

- Education seminars
- Benevolent Fund
- Annual dividends on shares
- Interest on deposits
