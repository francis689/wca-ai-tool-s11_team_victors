RUPSA SACCO - LOAN INFORMATION

GENERAL LOAN ELIGIBILITY

- A member must have saved consistently for at least 6 months.
- The member must maintain a good credit history with the SACCO.
- Loan application forms must be submitted physically or digitally before the 5th day of every month for processing.

EMERGENCY LOAN

- Maximum amount: Ksh 200,000.
- Interest rate: 1.3% monthly.
- Maximum repayment period: 24 months.

SCHOOL FEES LOAN

- Maximum amount: Ksh 200,000.
- Interest rate: 1.15% monthly.
- Maximum repayment period: 12 months.

NORMAL LOAN

- Members can borrow up to 3 times their total savings deposits.
- Interest rate: 1.0% monthly.
- Maximum repayment period: 36 months.

SUPER LOAN

- Members can borrow up to 4 times their total deposits.
- Interest rate: 1.2% monthly.
- Maximum repayment period: 60 months.

M-CASH MOBILE LOAN

- Maximum amount: Ksh 50,000.
- Interest: Flat 5% upfront fee, not monthly amortized.
- Maximum repayment period: 1 month.

LAND AND CONSTRUCTION LOAN

- Maximum borrowing amount: 3 or 4 times the member's deposits.
- Interest rate: 1.4% monthly.
- Maximum repayment period: 72 months.
