RUPSA SACCO - MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS

To become a member:

- Fill out the membership application form.
- Provide 2 passport photos.
- Provide a copy of your National ID.
- Provide a copy of your KRA PIN certificate.
- Make a minimum initial contribution of Ksh 4,000.

The initial Ksh 4,000 consists of:
- Ksh 1,000 non-refundable entrance fee.
- Ksh 1,000 Benevolent Fund fee.
- Ksh 2,000 goes to member deposits.

SHARE CAPITAL

- Minimum share capital: Ksh 10,000.
- The minimum share capital should be paid within 6 months.

MONTHLY CONTRIBUTION

- Individual members contribute Ksh 500 per month subsequently.
