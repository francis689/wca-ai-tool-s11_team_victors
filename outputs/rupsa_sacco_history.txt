QUESTION: What are the requirements of becoming a member of RUPSA SACCO?
ANSWER: To become a member of RUPSA SACCO, you need to meet the following requirements:

**Required Documents & Application:**
* Fill out the membership application form.
* Provide 2 passport photos.
* Provide a copy of your National ID.
* Provide a copy of your KRA PIN certificate.

**Financial Requirements:**
* **Minimum Initial Contribution:** Ksh 4,000, which is broken down as follows:
  * Ksh 1,000 non-refundable entrance fee
  * Ksh 1,000 Benevolent Fund fee
  * Ksh 2,000 goes to member deposits
* **Share Capital:** Minimum of Ksh 10,000, which should be paid within 6 months.
* **Subsequent Monthly Contribution:** Individual members contribute Ksh 500 per month going forward.

**Next Steps:**
To get started, complete the membership application form and submit it together with your 2 passport photos, a copy of your National ID, a copy of your KRA PIN certificate, and the minimum initial contribution of Ksh 4,000.
